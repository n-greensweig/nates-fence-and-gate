Craftsman

Our Craftsman-style fencing is a customizable design that can feature an open picket, side-by-side, and overlapping pattern. These are great 'good neighbor' fences because they are built to look the same from either side, often adorned with three decorative trim boards and the option of adding cap boards on top as well.
